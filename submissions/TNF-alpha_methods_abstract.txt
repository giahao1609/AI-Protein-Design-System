Methods Abstract for TNF-alpha Binder Design (v1.0_early_claim):
1. Target Preparation: Target sequence sliced strictly according to official UniProt
annotations. Disordered terminal overhangs and signal peptides masked from scoring.
2. De Novo Architecture: Candidates engineered de novo via generative pipelines
(VHH, strictly within 100-150 aa). Zero template grafting.
3. In Silico Confidence & ipSAE: Complex geometries evaluated via ESMFold2 under competition
specs (1 seed, 1 sample, no templates; target MSA enabled, binder single-sequence mode).
Candidates prioritized for high predicted interface confidence (ipSAE), broad sequence diversity,
and favorable developability profiles for downstream experimental screening.
4. Quality & Druggability: Sequences contain only 20 natural amino acids. Screened against
purification tags, unpaired cysteines, N-glycosylation, charge imbalance, and degradation motifs.
